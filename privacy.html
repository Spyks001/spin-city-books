Spin City Books Privacy Policy

Spin City Books is the bookkeeping application for Spin City Laundry Services.

Information we process

The application processes account information, business orders, expenses, team access information, profile photos, and M-PESA payment information needed to provide cloud bookkeeping and access control.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, operate bookkeeping features, synchronize business records, manage staff access, and display payment records received through the configured M-PESA integration.

Service providers

Spin City Books uses Supabase for authentication and cloud database services and GitHub Pages for hosting the web application.

Data retention and deletion

Business records are retained according to the business owner's operational needs. Account or record deletion requests can be made to the business owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Spin City Laundry Services through the business contact details shown in the application.
